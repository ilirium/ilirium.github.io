👨🏻‍💻

Hey, I am Ilya Kalimulin, I'm a senior machine learning engineer with eight years in industry. PhD in electronic engineering, three years as a research fellow before that, co-founded a startup back then.

I spent four and a half years at OpenWeather on satellite and radar imagery and weather data. I developed a service for crop-type segmentation on satellite time series, trained several CNN models. And some backend work: I got seven times the throughput out of the in-house multi-terabyte image store by exploiting the file format internals.

For the last three years at EyeQ Imaging I've been on production photo enhancement. Two things I'd point to. I improved production face detection, cutting errors in hard cases — rotation, profiles, large groups, fake faces, by working out the logic to combine four models' outputs. And I built the glasses-glare-removal pipeline end to end: trained the lens segmentation and glare classifiers, built normalized face crop extraction and orientation correction, orchestrated several teammates' models into one pipeline, converted everything to ONNX, and shipped it as an AWS service on Lambda, SQS and EC2.

The common thread in my work is productionising research: taking something that works in a notebook and making it run unattended at volume.


🎓

I completed my Ph.D. degree in 2014 in the edge of microwave engineering and electronic design automation. Once I gave an oral speech at a scientific conference in San Jose (California, USA): [Application of CMA evolution strategy to synthesis of multi-resonance SPICE models up to 20 GHz](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7347123).


🏡

Currently, my family and I are living in Florianópolis, Brazil (UTC-3). My wife and I have 2 brilliant small kids. Before Brazil, we had the experience of living in India (Goa), European Union (Latvia), and Turkey. Originally we are from Russia.
