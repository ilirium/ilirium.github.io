Today’s memory prices are unlikely to last. Even now, a laptop with 128 GB of unified RAM is less expensive than it may seem. Apple has sparked a quiet shift in RAM architecture, with Nvidia and AMD following through products like DGX Spark and Strix Halo.

I expect that within the next five years, 1024 GB of unified RAM will become reasonably affordable—a modest investment for a powerful workhorse machine. Local models and open-source harnesses are advancing quickly, and model architecture is improving as well through techniques such as Mixture of Experts and instruction-following pruning.

JetBrains IDEs already use local models to power inline code completion.

I’ve only recently begun exploring local LLMs and local agents, but I can already see a few promising personal projects to build.
