Revolut, one of the biggest neobanks, is down worldwide for several hours. I hope it is not because GPT-9 decided to find details on how Revolut's LLM PRAGMA works internally :)

According to their status page (https://www.revolut.com/system-status/), all their systems are having problems. Card payments through Apple Pay work, I just checked, but I cannot see the transaction in the app. Their support bot also doesn't work.

I hope they recover, and I hope they write a good postmortem, especially what they did and how they used any AI tools. Thanks, I have different banking cards in my wallet: Nubank, Wise, etc.

But let's imagine another situation: AWS us-east-1 went down, and OpenAI or Anthropic did too. It can be a disaster. So, is it interesting? Do big tech companies simulate this kind of situation? I know Google regularly simulates situations where some of its data centers are down. But modern realities need more, ahem, corporate war games.
