Will AI replace humans? As someone who has worked in AI development for the past eight years, I’d like to share my perspective.

Companies are cutting staff because the financial crisis has arrived—and in many cases, it was overdue, since some organizations had become bloated. AI will automate many tasks, and companies will eventually find a new staffing balance: fewer people would be insufficient, while more would be unnecessary. 

Once AI is in place and that balance is reached, growth will still depend on competing for customers, sales, and profit. And how will companies do that? With people. If one person using AI can produce X amount of work, then 10 people can produce roughly X × 0.9 × 10.

The same pattern played out in the 1980s and 1990s. As computers became widespread, accounting departments with 500 people shrank to a few dozen accountants using DBase, SAP, and Excel. At the same time, IT grew from nothing into a major field.

Something similar is happening now, some professions and functions are being automated, while AI grew from nothing into a major field.
