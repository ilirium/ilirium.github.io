How can AI Labs make money?

Do you remember Clubhouse? Early in the COVID pandemic, there was a lot of talk about how Clubhouse could start making money. Do Frontier AI Labs have monetization beyond paying for tokens? Let’s help them shape their revenue and not blame them for trying to ban open-source models.

Pay for:
- better guardrails
- turning off all restriction
- security capabilities
- ???
- PROFIT

Today, Microsoft is one of the biggest contributors to open source, but two decades ago they used to pay for marketing campaigns against Linux.

It is clear why frontiers play against open-source models. Should the US ban them? But if they are sourced from the EU?

Why did Microsoft stop FUDding OSS? They integrated it into their revenue pipelines, and it helps them to grow and, actually, to diversify their business.
What lessons can frontiers learn from this?

We can help them to find more ways to make money: their focus on selling tokens is understandable – it was the easiest way for early years. But for today, there are far more revenue streams.

For McDonald's, it is land ownership. For Coca-Cola, it is selling syrup B2B.

My first obvious idea is to sell security specialized agents B2B/B2G for a higher margin. Another obvious idea: relax restrictions for those who want, again, at much higher tiers. Create better guardrails, sandboxing, AI firewalling, SELinux for LLM, modern "sticky bits".

Acquire OS models internally. People want them? Give them. You have know-how to cheaply serve models: do this. Better to take this money in your hands than in your competitors' pockets, yeah? Integrate even local models. 

Do business, do integrations, not speculate on politics.

Who will be the first to follow this direction?
