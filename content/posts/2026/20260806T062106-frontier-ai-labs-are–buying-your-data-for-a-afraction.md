Data is everything. To keep on track, Frontier AI Labs should feed their models with better, new, unseen data. Where to get them? From their APIs users.

Every coding session under human supervision is valuable. To improve the coding harness, it is needed to know what in the workflow is working and what is not.

Generating the next token works perfectly. The next upgrade is not in this. The next progress is in behavior: what humans expect and what they do not. There is only one way to get this data – to learn from real sessions with real humans. Not from books. Not from social media.

Subsidizing is a way to attract clients. But also, a way to buy your interactions for a fraction. Feeding this interaction steadily improves frontier models' progress. It is good. But for you, it means that you are giving them the soul of our business processes.

Building coding agent loops against frontier APIs? Renting your software engineers to them for a fraction of your real cost.

Go for local models. Build coding agent loops with them. Keep engineering expertise in your team, not on the frontier side.

Don't allow them to buy years of your business expertise and vision for a fraction. Find a way to integrate local open-source models into routines and processes.
